2026 CHAOS Security 6G_NETWORKS

Chaotic Cryptography: Foundations, Applications,and Integration with Quantum, IoT, and 6G

This research presents CHAOS, an advanced cybersecurity framework designed to improve threat detection, incident analysis, and security intelligence through the integration of artificial intelligence and behavioral analytics. The study focuses on identifying malicious activities across complex environments, correlating security events, and providing actionable insights to strengthen organizational cyber resilience.

Key Contributions

  • Enhanced security event correlation and threat investigation capabilities
  • Scalable framework for modern SOC operations and proactive cyber defense
  • Integration of AI-assisted decision support for faster incident response and threat prioritization
IEEE PUBLISHED
2025 QUANTUM_CRYPTO 6G_NETWORKS

Quantum Cryptography in 6G & AI-Driven Security

This research explores how quantum-resistant cryptographic methods can be embedded into 6G network architectures to secure next-generation ultra-low-latency communication. The study focuses on integrating Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) with AI-based optimization models to dynamically manage key lifecycles, reduce entropy weaknesses, and enhance end-to-end encryption scalability.

Key Contributions

  • Measurable improvements in secure channel establishment speeds
  • Enhanced post-quantum threat resistance frameworks
  • Intelligent, self-healing communication network architectures

AI-Driven Digital Twin for IoT Security: Generative Attacks and Self-Healing

Design and analysis of digital-twin models that replicate real network environments to simulate cyberattacks and defensive strategies in safe, controlled ecosystems. These twins integrate AI-driven threat modeling, behavioral anomaly detection, and closed-loop feedback systems to improve SOC decision-making and incident readiness.

Innovation Areas

  • Predictive defense enabling pre-attack vulnerability testing
  • Countermeasure validation in simulated environments
  • Resilience strengthening before real-world deployment
